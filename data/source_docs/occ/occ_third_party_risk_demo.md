# OCC Demo Note: Third-Party Risk Management

This demo note is provided so the application can be tested from a clean clone. It is not official OCC guidance.

Bank third-party risk management programs should support due diligence, contract review, ongoing monitoring, issue management, and documentation that allows management and examiners to understand the risk posture of important vendor relationships.

Useful evidence for review may include:

- due diligence records
- risk assessments
- contract terms and service-level expectations
- monitoring results
- issue logs and remediation status
- management reporting

Audit-ready reporting should preserve source documents, timestamps, reviewer decisions, and open issues so that conclusions can be traced back to underlying evidence.
